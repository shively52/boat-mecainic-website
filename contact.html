Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a service appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling us at (239) 555-0123, filling out our contact form, or visiting our facility during business hours.

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency boat repair services. Call our emergency line at (239) 555-0124 for immediate assistance.

How long does a typical service take?

Service duration varies depending on the type of service. Routine maintenance typically takes 2-4 hours, while more complex repairs may take several days. We'll provide an estimated timeframe when you schedule your appointment.

Do you service all boat brands and types?

Yes, we service all major boat brands and types, from small fishing boats to luxury yachts. Our certified technicians are trained to work on both inboard and outboard engines.

What should I bring for my service appointment?

Please bring your boat's service history, any relevant documentation, and a list of concerns or issues you'd like us to address. If you're experiencing specific problems, it's helpful to describe them in detail.
