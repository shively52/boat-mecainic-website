About Us

Your trusted partner in boat maintenance and care since 2005

Our Story

Fort Myers Boat Services was founded in 2005 by marine mechanic John Smith, who recognized a need for reliable and professional boat maintenance services in the Fort Myers area. What started as a small operation has grown into one of the most trusted boat service providers in Southwest Florida.

With over 15 years of experience, our team of certified technicians has serviced thousands of vessels, from small fishing boats to luxury yachts. We take pride in our workmanship and customer service, ensuring that every boat owner receives personalized attention and expert care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide exceptional boat maintenance and detailing services that exceed our customers' expectations. We are committed to:

  • Delivering high-quality workmanship on every job
  • Using only the best products and equipment
  • Providing transparent pricing and honest assessments
  • Ensuring customer satisfaction through attentive service
  • Maintaining the highest standards of professionalism
